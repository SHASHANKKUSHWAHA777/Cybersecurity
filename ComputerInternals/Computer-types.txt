Types of computer
1. Laptop - It has a screen and a keyboard . Portable computing everyday
2. Desktop - screen and keyboard . sustained performance at a fixed location.
3. Workstation - Screen and keyboard . Precision and reliability for professional tasks.
4. Server - No Screen and no keyboard . Providing services to many users over a network

Now coming to small computers that are powerful and are handy we can fit inside our pockets
1. Smartphone - pocketsized comp for battery and connectivity . eg iphone and android
2. Tablet - Touch comp with large screen . eg ipad , drawing tablet 
3. IoT device - Network connected device for a single purpose . eg thermostat , smart doorbell , fitness tracker etc
4. Embedded Computer - Computer built into another device . eg coffee maker controller , automatic door sensor
